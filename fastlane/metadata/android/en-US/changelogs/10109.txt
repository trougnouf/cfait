### 💼 Other

- *(release)* Split build-windows from publish
- Store settings as collection properties with VEVENT fallback ( https://github.com/trougnouf/cfait/issues/91 )

Synchronize the Cfait settings payload as custom WebDAV properties
(CF:settings / CF:settings-rev in the http://trougnouf.com/ns/cfait/
namespace) on the default calendar collection, after probing for
arbitrary-property support with a temporary CF:probe property. On
servers that reject custom properties, fall back to a 1970 CANCELLED
VEVENT carrier that most calendar clients filter out by time range.
A deleted or changed target collection is healed by re-provisioning
the property on the new target and removing the stale properties from
the old one. Includes unit tests, ICS round-trip tests and mockito
integration tests, plus a fix for CATEGORIES parsing on VEVENTs.
